## Project Title
CampusConnect: University Event Management System

## Problem Statement
Students often miss out on university events and club activities due to scattered information across different notice boards and groups.

## Objective
To provide a centralized platform for students to explore, register for, and track university events and club activities using a structured HTML-only website.

## Target Users
University Students, Faculty, and Club Organizers.

## Planned Pages & Features
Total of 15 pages (5 pages per member) focusing purely on HTML semantics, forms, tables, lists, and interconnected navigation without CSS/JS.
